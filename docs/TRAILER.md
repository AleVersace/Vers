## What the footage shows

The table interactions come from Vers at commit
[`56393b6`](https://github.com/AleVersace/Vers/commit/56393b646ef67e5d00870c29cb8518e41efbba03),
using an isolated local demo and an in-memory data adapter. The scene,
characters, statistics and map were created for the trailer. They do not come
from a private campaign. The footage is not a demonstration of multi-user
synchronization or an invitation to the maintainer's personal installation.

The grid uses one-cell tokens. The advantage roll is an actual app result:
16 and 1, choose 16, add the bonus once (+6), total 22. Close-ups were rendered
larger by the browser before capture to keep the text readable; the result was
not retyped or changed in editing. Browser captures at about 5–7 frames per
second were edited into a 30 fps presentation with titles and transitions.

The map was generated for the trailer with imagegen, without reference images;
the token emblems were created for this demonstration. Avenir Next and
Baskerville appear as rendered text; font files are not distributed. The local
editing project, capture fixture and standalone music track are not part of
this public media release.
