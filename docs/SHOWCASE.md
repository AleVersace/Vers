## Try it at your table

[Try the browser demo](https://aleversace.itch.io/vers) · [Set up your own multiplayer table](DEPLOYMENT.md) · [Follow Vers on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@VersTabletop)

reload. For multiplayer and saved campaigns, follow the setup guide.
